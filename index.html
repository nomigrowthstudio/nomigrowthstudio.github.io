How it works

One system from enquiry to viewing.

Everything runs inside your own Google account: Sheets holds the lead list, Gmail sends the emails, and a small automation script does the rest. You keep full control, and we keep no copy of your data.

  • One lead sheet

    Website and portal enquiries land in one Google Sheet with name, phone, budget and source.

  • Instant reply

    The buyer gets an email within seconds confirming you received the enquiry.

  • WhatsApp alerts

    Agents get an alert with a one-tap WhatsApp button that opens the chat with a greeting already typed.

  • Automatic follow-ups

    Follow-ups on day 1, 3 and 7 to the buyer, the agent or both, until the status changes.

  • Morning summary

    At 9 am the owner sees new leads, leads nobody has called yet, and follow-ups due today.

  • Portal capture

    Lead alert emails from Zameen, Bayut or Property Finder are read and added to the sheet automatically.

FAQ

Questions agencies ask first.

Something else on your mind? Ask it in the free chat.

Do I need to buy any software?

No. It runs on a Google account, which is free. A free Gmail account can send about 100 automated emails a day, and Google Workspace accounts about 1,500, which covers most agencies.

Who owns the system and the leads?

You do. Everything is installed in your Google account. After handover we have no access unless you invite us.

Does it work with Zameen, Bayut and Property Finder?

It reads the lead alert emails these portals send you and adds each enquiry to your sheet. We test it on your real alerts during setup.

Can it send WhatsApp messages by itself?

Your agents get a one-tap WhatsApp link with the message already typed, so the chat starts from their own number. Fully automatic WhatsApp sending needs the paid WhatsApp Business API, which we can add later if you want it.

Do you work outside Pakistan?

Yes. We work with agencies in Pakistan, the UAE and the UK. Messages and calls happen in your time zone.

How do payments work?

50% after you approve the free demo, 50% when the system goes live. Bank transfer in USD, AED, GBP or PKR.

What if something breaks?

Fixes are free for 30 days after go-live. After that, the Care plan covers changes and support, or you can pay per fix.
